The team held a sprint meeting to check progress ahead of the release date. The main problems were login failures caused by an authentication and token management issue, and mixed feedback on the new UI color schemes. Wan, George and the speaker will work on the authentication bugs, while the speaker will also refine the color schemes and finish the UI adjustments. A few authentication edge cases remain, so the team will document them as known issues in the release notes and plan post-release patches. During the demo there was a problem with the login, and the team switched to the older version to avoid further complications, making the authentication fix the top priority before launch. The team will also update the support documentation, tell users about the fix after release, hold a post-release review meeting, and reconvene tomorrow to review progress.
